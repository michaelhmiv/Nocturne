# Terminal-intent planning

Nocturne routes a command by the player's terminal intent rather than by the first supporting motion or incidental noun.

The affordance adjudicator runs before planning and classifies asserted premises as established, plausible ephemeral, scene-local, persistent-required, contradictory, or uncertain. The planner must preserve the adjudicated terminal intent as `primaryKind`, while prerequisite steps may use other handlers. For example, travel followed by an attack remains a combat plan with an arrival dependency; eating gum from a light pole remains consumption rather than search.

Ephemeral premises may support one immediate low-impact resolution without creating inventory, durable entities, or persistent identifiers. Advantage-bearing, identity-bearing, contradictory, and uncertain premises require authoritative evidence, legitimate search or materialization, or clarification.
